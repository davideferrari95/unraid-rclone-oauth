Unraid Rclone Backup

This is a personal, non-commercial OAuth application used by one operator to connect the open-source rclone tool to the operator's Google Drive account for backups of an Unraid NAS.

What the application does

After the operator explicitly authorizes Google Drive access, rclone reads, uploads, updates, lists, and, when required by configured retention rules, deletes backup files in that account. The application is not offered to third parties and does not operate a publicly accessible backup service.

How Google data is used

Google Drive files and their metadata are accessed only to maintain and verify the operator's backups. OAuth credentials and operational logs are stored within systems controlled by the operator, not in this public website or its repository.

For details about data access, storage, retention, and deletion, read the Privacy Policy.

This personal integration is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Google, rclone, or Unraid.
